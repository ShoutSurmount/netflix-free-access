## 🍿 The End Of Subscription Fatigue

In 2026, the average household subscribes to four different streaming services, paying over $60 a month just to watch their favorite shows. Netflix, while the king of content, keeps raising its prices and cracking down on password sharing. It is time for a different approach.

**Netflix Free Access** is a comprehensive, open-source repository dedicated to unlocking the Netflix catalog without the monthly fee. This is not a shady website filled with malware and pop-ups. It is a meticulously engineered toolkit that leverages the open-source community's collective power to bypass regional locks, ad-supported tiers, and paywalls.

This repository provides a complete suite of tools. It includes a custom DNS configuration guide to unlock all regional libraries (watch US content from Europe, or Japanese anime from the US). It also includes a lightweight web proxy that strips out the "Premium" enforcement scripts from the Netflix web player. It is the ultimate companion for the cord-cutting purist who refuses to pay the Netflix tax.

## 🌍 The Three Keys To Free Access

We have structured the toolkit around three core modules, each addressing a different layer of Netflix's restrictions.

### Module 1: The DNS Unlocker
Netflix uses geo-blocking to restrict content. If you are in the UK, you cannot watch US exclusive shows. Our toolkit provides a curated list of smart DNS servers. By changing your device's DNS settings to point to our servers, the Netflix app thinks you are in a different country. This unlocks thousands of hidden movies and TV shows.

### Module 2: The Ad-Stripper Proxy
Netflix introduced a cheaper "Ad-Supported" tier. However, the ads are incredibly annoying and unskippable. This module is a local proxy (written in Python) that intercepts the Netflix video manifest files. It detects the ad segments and automatically skips them, giving you an ad-free experience on the budget tier.

### Module 3: The Cookie Injector
For users who want to bypass the login screen entirely, this tool provides a library of shared "cookies." These are temporary sessions generated by community members. By injecting a valid cookie into your browser, you can browse the Netflix library anonymously without creating an account.

## 🧠 Under The Hood: How The Bypass Works

Netflix is incredibly secure. You cannot simply "hack" your way into a free account. So how do these methods work?

The **DNS Unlocker** works because Netflix's geo-blocking is based on your IP address. When you type `netflix.com`, your DNS server translates that into an IP address. By using our smart DNS, we return an IP address that is located in the US (or another region). Netflix sees a US IP and serves you the US library.

The **Ad-Stripper Proxy** is a man-in-the-middle attack. It sits between your browser and Netflix's servers. When your browser requests a video, the proxy intercepts the request. If the video stream includes ad segments, the proxy edits the manifest file to remove them before sending it back to your browser. Netflix's servers are none the wiser.
